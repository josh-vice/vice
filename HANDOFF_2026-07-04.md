  app shell heights (.app-layout/.sidebar/.main-wrap), search dropdown 60vh, glossary lightbox 92vh,

  `LTUtils.echartsZoomShim(host)` — capture-phase listener shadows offsetX/offsetY/layerX/layerY
  (mouse+pointer+wheel; touch untouched) → zrender falls back to its marker transform solver → exact
  (verified zrX 772.06). Wired at all 3 echarts.init sites (engine renderChart, sim chart, sim equity).
