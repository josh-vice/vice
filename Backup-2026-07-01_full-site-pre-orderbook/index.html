Learn by doing — not by watching.

        
Every concept is paired with real, interactive charts and tools so it actually sticks. All of it is free to use.

      

      

        

          

            

            
Practice Simulator

            
Place trades on live-feeling charts with draggable take-profit and stop-loss orders. Build instincts and rehearse risk management — completely risk-free, no real money.

          

          

        

        
Interactive Charts
Every lesson is built on real candlestick charts you can read, replay, and learn to anticipate.

        
Glossary & Search
80+ cross-referenced terms with diagrams. Search any concept instantly from anywhere in the app.

        
Flashcards
Per-course decks to drill the vocabulary, then review only the ones you got wrong.

        
Exam & Certificate
Test yourself with a final exam and earn a printable certificate of completion.
