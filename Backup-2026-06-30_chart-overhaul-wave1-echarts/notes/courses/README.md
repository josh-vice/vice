## How to Use This Material

### Suggested Study Path

**Week 1–2:** DVD 1 only  
Read the course, then watch the video, then read again. Before moving on, be able to answer all 5 study questions from memory.

**Week 3–4:** DVD 2  
Same process. Focus especially on the opening types — these are immediately actionable.

**Week 5–6:** DVD 3  
Study balance and excess until they feel intuitive. Practice identifying them on historical charts.

**Week 7–8:** DVD 4  
Write a narrative every day before the market opens, even if you're not trading. Do this for 30 days.

**Month 3+:** Review everything together.  
The concepts are fractal and reinforce each other. Every re-read reveals something that wasn't obvious the first time.

## Dalton's 10 Most Important Quotes

1. *"At its foundation, the market is simply a two-way auction process."*

2. *"The Point of Control is the fairest price at which business is being conducted."*

3. *"We trade value, not price. Price is an advertising mechanism."*

4. *"Excess marks the end of one auction and the beginning of another."*

5. *"Short-term balance is absolute paradise for short-term traders — but traders do what works until it doesn't work anymore."*

6. *"Markets are irrational — but irrational in a predictable manner."*

7. *"Trade location is the best tool available for risk control."*

8. *"Without preparation, we are unlikely to overcome the emotions that really cost us dearly in the market."*

9. *"We can control risk. We cannot control return."*

10. *"You will probably never look at the market the same way again."*
